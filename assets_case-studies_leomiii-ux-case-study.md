# Case Study: Leomiii E-Commerce Redesign

**Role:** Lead UI/UX Designer  
**Timeline:** 4 Weeks  
**Tools:** Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop, WooCommerce

## 1. Project Overview
Leomiii is a modern women's apparel brand specializing in western dresses, including maxis, sundresses, wraps, and fringe designs. The primary objective of this project was to overhaul the existing e-commerce experience to reduce cart abandonment and create a seamless, visually appealing shopping journey, especially on mobile devices.

## 2. The Problem
Analytics revealed that users were experiencing friction during the checkout process, resulting in a high drop-off rate immediately after adding items to their cart. The previous interface lacked a clear visual hierarchy, making the transition from browsing vibrant dresses to the payment gateway feel disconnected and cumbersome.

## 3. Design Process & Research
* **User Personas:** Developed profiles focusing on fashion-forward female shoppers who value aesthetics and a fast, intuitive mobile shopping experience.
* **Competitive Analysis:** Evaluated the checkout flows of top apparel competitors to identify industry standards for minimizing friction.
* **User Flow Mapping:** Redesigned the purchasing path to a streamlined "Product Page" -> "Slide-out Cart" -> "One-Page Checkout" model.

### High-Fidelity UI
The final interface introduced a clean, minimalist aesthetic with ample white space, allowing the colorful western apparel to stand out.
* **Color Palette:** Neutral background tones to provide contrast for the vibrant product photos.
* **Typography:** A clean, modern sans-serif font selected for maximum readability on small mobile screens.
* **Interactions:** Added micro-interactions for adding items to the cart to provide immediate visual feedback without reloading the page.

## 5. Impact & Learnings
The redesigned WooCommerce integration resulted in a much smoother user experience. By implementing a one-page checkout and highly visible calls-to-action, the purchasing flow became intuitive and frictionless.

**Key Takeaway:** Effective e-commerce design requires balancing brand aesthetics with rigorous usability standards to remove every possible barrier between the user and their purchase.
